# Quit It Support

For support, privacy questions, or data requests, email [orkun.hacilar@gmail.com](mailto:orkun.hacilar@gmail.com).

## Common help

- **Restore a purchase:** Open Quit It, go to the paywall or Help & Support, and choose Restore Purchases.
- **Manage a subscription:** Use the subscription settings of the Apple App Store or Google Play account used for purchase.
- **Delete local data:** Open Settings → Data & Privacy and choose Delete All App Data.
- **Disable usage analytics:** Open Settings → Privacy and turn off Usage Analytics.
- **Export data:** Open Settings → Data & Privacy and choose Export.

Quit It has no user account system. Do not send private journey notes or sensitive health information in a support email.

# Quit It Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — Last updated September 27, 2026

Quit It is a local-first self-tracking app intended primarily for adults. It does not require an account. Journeys, habit information, motivations, dates, check-ins, setbacks, notes, triggers, milestones, savings/time settings, reminders, and preferences are stored in the app sandbox on the user's device and are not uploaded to a Quit It backend.

## Analytics and diagnostics

When Usage Analytics is enabled, Firebase Analytics receives limited product events such as feature interactions, generalized status values, store product outcomes, sanitized campaign labels, and milestone identifiers. Private free text, raw journey history, notification text, contact data, advertising identifiers, and raw URLs are excluded. Usage Analytics can be disabled in Settings → Privacy.

Firebase Crashlytics may process crash logs, stack traces, and technical app/device/install/session metadata. Firebase Remote Config processes technical service metadata to provide non-critical configuration.

## Purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes payment. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app-specific customer identifier, product and entitlement state, purchase or store receipt metadata, and technical information needed to provide and restore Premium. Quit It does not send private journey content to RevenueCat.

## Notifications, widgets, export, and deletion

Notification schedules and widget data are local. Private wording is used by default. Export is generated locally and is shared only through a destination explicitly chosen by the user. Users can delete individual journeys or all local app data from within the app. Local deletion does not cancel a store subscription or delete Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Firebase Analytics, or Crashlytics records already processed under those providers' retention rules.

## Security and tracking

App data is protected by the operating-system sandbox and device security. Optional App Lock uses native device authentication; Quit It never receives biometric templates. Network SDK traffic uses encrypted connections. Quit It does not request advertising identifiers, use cross-company tracking, or sell personal data.
